Learn the Unit

Lessons

The lessons in this section guide students through the main ideas in the Application of Vectors unit, with clear explanations, examples, and visual support.

Lesson Topics

Four complete lessons built from our instructional slide decks. Each overview lists the exact skills covered and their alignment with the CCT learning requirements (G1–G7).

01

Vector Fundamentals and Operations

Build the foundations for describing, representing, and combining vectors in two and three dimensions.

Topics Covered

  • Scalar vs. vector quantities; magnitude and direction
  • Vector notation, geometric vectors, and equivalent vectors
  • Algebraic vectors and magnitudes in 2D and 3D
  • Geometric and algebraic vector addition and subtraction
  • Scalar multiplication and its effect on direction and magnitude
  • Resultant forces and vector resolution into components

Learning Focus: Foundations and vector operations

CCT Coverage: G1 · G2 · G3 · part of G4

02

Vector Applications: Navigation

Apply vector addition and subtraction to real travel problems involving aircraft, boats, winds, and river currents.

Topics Covered

  • Velocity and relative velocity
  • Resultant velocity and vector diagrams
  • Airplane navigation: airspeed, wind speed, and ground speed
  • Heading, true direction, and bearings
  • Boat navigation: river currents, downstream drift, and crossing time
  • Heading corrections to reach a destination directly
  • Navigation calculations using sine and cosine rules

Learning Focus: Real-world navigation applications

CCT Coverage: G7

03

Dot Product and Its Applications

Learn how the dot product connects vector magnitudes, angles, and components, then use it in geometry and physics.

Topics Covered

  • Geometric dot product and the effect of the included angle
  • Algebraic dot product in 2D and 3D
  • Properties: commutative, distributive, scalar multiplication, and self-dot product
  • Angles between vectors and tests for perpendicularity
  • Geometric applications, including parallelogram diagonals
  • Scalar and vector projections using geometric and algebraic methods
  • Direction cosines and corresponding direction angles
  • Mechanical work calculated with the dot product

Learning Focus: Dot product and its applications

CCT Coverage: part of G4 · G5

Lesson Slides (PDF)

04

Cross Product and Its Applications

Use the cross product to find perpendicular directions and solve geometric and rotational force problems.

Topics Covered

  • Geometric cross product: magnitude and direction
  • Algebraic cross product in 3D; embedding 2D vectors in 3D
  • Right-hand rule and anti-commutative property
  • Parallel vectors and the zero cross product
  • Vectors perpendicular to two vectors; normals to planes
  • Areas of parallelograms and triangles using cross products
  • Torque: magnitude, direction, units, and the right-hand rule

Learning Focus: Cross product and its applications

CCT Coverage: part of G4 · G6
